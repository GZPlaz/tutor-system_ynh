MindAccel Tutoring is a complete tutoring-centre platform. Everything is free for students — there are no payments, plans or paywalls.

- **Booking** — students (or visitors, if you allow it) pick a subject, tutor and time; tutors confirm; everyone gets emails and reminders; sessions run on Google Meet.
- **People** — students, tutors and administrators with custom roles and permissions; sign-ups can be open, approved by an admin, or invitation only.
- **Resource Hub** — tutors share files and links with students or whole classes.
- **Classes** — group students and assign resources to the whole group.
- **Branding** — name, logo, colours and wording are edited in the app.
- **Settings** — sign-up rules, booking rules and a maintenance switch, changed in the app without touching the server.
